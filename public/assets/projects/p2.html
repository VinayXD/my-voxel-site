    • Farmers’ Market: A Strategic and Deceptive Card Game

    Welcome to Farmers' Market, where you take on the role of a savvy farmer competing against rivals to sell all your animals and head home first. This fast-paced, strategy-driven game challenges players to bluff, call out lies, and create sequences to secure victory. This project introduces the fundamentals of game design, exploring both physical and digital games. It covers game elements, development from concept to prototype, and playtesting to final design.

      Game Components

      Farm Animal Cards

      Features nine types of animals in four colors. The back of each card has a sealed crate image.

      Power Cards

      Special cards that act as wildcards to complete sequences.

      Color Tokens

      Used to indicate the announced color of the card in play.

      Auction Hammer Card

      Marks the starting player for each round.

    Gameplay Overview

    The game alternates between two phases:

    1.Auction Phase:

    The auctioneer (player with the hammer card) nominates an animal by playing a card face-down and declaring its type and color. Players take turns either playing matching cards, bluffing, or passing.

    2.Action Phase:

    Players can call out bluffs, take cards, or pass. Outcomes depend on whether suspicions are correct, influencing the flow of cards and strategies.

    Winning the Game:

    The game ends when a player: Discards all their cards. or last remianing cards are valid sequence of cards (e.g., three or more consecutively numbered cards of the same color).

    Key Features

    Mechanics:

    Decide when to lie or call out opponents for high-risk, high-reward gameplay.

    Power Cards:

    Use these strategically to complete sequences and gain an edge.

    Dynamic Rounds:

    The auction hammer rotates, ensuring every player has a chance to lead and control the game’s pace.
